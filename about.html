Why this exists

An estimated 58,000+ people die from snakebite in India every year — around half of all snakebite deaths worldwide — and the large majority are preventable with timely antivenom. Source: Suraweera et al., "Trends in snakebite deaths in India from 2000 to 2019," eLife, 2020 — a nationally representative mortality study analyzing over 600,000 verbal autopsies.

The gap this app targets

After a snakebite, three things usually go wrong at once: it's often unclear what kind of snake was involved, it's unclear which nearby hospital actually has the right antivenom in stock right now, and precious time is lost figuring both out. Most snakebite deaths in India happen in rural areas, often because people couldn't find a hospital with antivenom in time — not because no treatment existed at all.

AntiVenom Finder connects three things that are normally disconnected: identifying the snake, finding nearby hospitals, and knowing which of them actually have antivenom in stock right now.

What we do differently

Operator-approved hospitals
Every hospital is reviewed before it appears in public search, to keep fake or unverified listings out.
Real-time stock reporting
Hospitals update their own antivenom inventory directly, with a visible freshness indicator.
Human-confirmed identification
Manual selection by someone who saw the snake, not an automated guess — more reliable for a life-or-death decision.
Prevention-first messaging
Most bites are preventable — see the Precautions page for practical, low-cost steps.

What this app is not

This is not a medical diagnosis tool and does not replace emergency services. Hospital stock is self-reported by each hospital and should be confirmed by phone before traveling. If you're facing an active snakebite emergency, call 108 or 112 (India) immediately, in addition to using this app.
